Our Story

Born in the Bayou,
Raised in Austin.

Cypress Grill is a love letter to the rich, layered culinary history of New Orleans, reimagined through the lens of Austin's contemporary dining scene. We believe that true luxury lies in the artisanal—the careful tending of a dark roux, the exact char on a piece of Gulf seafood, the subtle notes of a perfectly aged bourbon.

Our space is designed to be an escape. When you step through our doors, the noise of the city fades, replaced by the clinking of crystal, the soft hum of jazz, and the enveloping warmth of our hospitality. This is not just a meal; it is an evening crafted with intention.
